# Ecommerce solution page: content strategy

Route: `/services/ecommerce`. Historical approved strategy; revised by the
[25 October content audit](2026-10-25-ecommerce-content-audit.md). That audit
and the implemented page take precedence where wording or section order differs.

## Goal

Turn product companies into booked calls. One primary action throughout:
**Book a call**. One lower-commitment action for visitors not ready to talk:
**Get a free redesign** (we redesign their homepage or one product page so
they can see how their store might look).

## Who it's for

Companies with a defined product line, often premium, technical, or
configurable (hardware, furniture, lighting, mobility, energy, audio), who are:

- taking the product online for the first time, or
- outgrowing a store and brand that no longer match the product.

Readers are founders, CEOs, and heads of marketing or ecommerce. Their worries:
juggling several agencies, a launch that drags, a beautiful site that does not
sell, and losing sales or search rankings during a switch.

## Positioning

We make products easy to understand and easy to buy: brand, 3D, configurators,
and a custom ecommerce store, created by one in-house team so the client can
stay focused on the product.

We offer custom ecommerce only; the page names no platform product.

### Message pillars (repeat across the page)

1. **Make it obvious.** 3D, motion, and clear product UX show what photos and
   spec sheets can't.
2. **Make it easy to buy.** Configurators that price and quote, checkouts that
   convert, and systems that connect to how the client sells.
3. **Make it look global.** A brand and store that stand next to category
   leaders.
4. **One team, all of it.** No hand-offs between vendors.

## Tone

- Short sentences; fragments are fine for rhythm.
- Talk about the client's product and customers more than about us.
- Specific nouns over adjectives: "CAD files, finishes, quotes", not
  "cutting-edge solutions".
- No hype words (world-class, revolutionary, seamless, unlock), no urgency
  tricks, no unsourced statistics.
- Confident understatement: say what we do, show the work, let clients make
  the claims.

The line in the brief, "Your product is complex. We make it obvious. 3D. Motion.
Branding. Product Design. Creative Development. Everything your product needs
to explain itself.", is Riotters' homepage hero word for word. This page uses
its own line in the same spirit.

## Visual direction

Product-led throughout: soft studio lighting, floating products, material
close-ups, and exploded views, in the spirit of the NOVA and Cornect Behance
references. Use real client renders rather than stock; grey placeholders stand
in until they arrive. Stay inside the existing design system: sky hero, dark
blue-lit panels, pill buttons.

### 3. Sound familiar?

Let visitors recognise themselves. Each situation maps to an outcome in
section 4.

1. **"Customers don't get it from the photos."** The best features are
   invisible in a gallery and buried in spec sheets.
2. **"Every custom order starts an email thread."** Options, prices, and quotes
   go back and forth with sales.
3. **"The brand looks smaller than the product."** Next to global competitors,
   the site undersells what you've built.
4. **"The store works against you."** Templates, plugins, and patches you've
   outgrown: slow, hard to change, and leaking sales.

Closing line: If one of these sounds like you, you're in the right place.

### 4. What changes

Outcomes, not capabilities. Each block has an outcome headline, one or two
lines, and a "Made with" row of tags that later links to the capability pages.

- **H2:** From first render to repeat order.

1. **Customers understand it in seconds.** Photoreal renders and motion show
   what's inside, how it works, and why it's better: technical films, animated
   features, 360° views. *Made with: 3D modelling, rendering, motion.*
2. **Custom orders that quote themselves.** Shoppers configure size, finish,
   and options in real-time 3D and see a price or request a quote on the spot.
   Your sales team receives a complete spec, not an email thread. *Made with:
   3D configurator, product configurator, pricing logic.*
3. **A brand that holds its own globally.** A new identity, or a careful
   rebrand that keeps what customers already trust, carried through product
   pages, packaging, and campaigns. *Made with: branding, art direction.*
4. **A store built around how you sell.** A custom storefront, admin, and app
   shaped around your products, connected to ERP, inventory, and payments where
   you need them. Fast for shoppers, simple for your team. *Made with: UX/UI,
   web and app development, ERP integration.*
5. **One set of visuals for every channel.** The same 3D assets power the
   store, ads, launch films, retail screens, and sales decks, without a new
   photoshoot for every colour. *Made with: 3D, motion, content.*
6. **Growth after launch.** We stay on for conversion testing, SEO, paid
   campaigns, and new product drops. *Made with: CRO, SEO, performance
   marketing.*

### 6. Why one team

The differentiator. Shows depth of expertise without bragging.

- **H2:** Most launches need five agencies. You need one team.
- **Body:** Brand designers, 3D artists, motion designers, product designers,
  engineers, and growth marketers work in one studio, on one plan. Your product
  files go in once; brand, visuals, configurator, and store come out
  consistent.
- **Three benefits:**
  - **One point of contact.** No relaying feedback between agencies.
  - **Quality that holds end to end.** The render, the configurator, and the ad
    share the same model, the same light, and the same brand.
  - **Your time back.** We handle the creative and the technical; you stay on
    the product and the business.

### 7. Process

Reduce perceived risk; show the work is organised.

- **H2:** From product files to first orders
1. **Discover.** Your product, your buyers, and your current store; we find
   what's costing you sales.
2. **Define.** Brand direction, product story, configurator rules, and pricing
   logic.
3. **Create.** 3D models from your CAD files or samples, renders, motion, and
   identity.
4. **Build.** Storefront, admin, configurator, and integrations, tested on real
   devices.
5. **Launch and grow.** Launch assets and campaigns, then ongoing
   optimisation.
- **Reassurance:** You see progress every week, with no big reveal at the end.
  Most projects launch in 1–3 months, depending on your product range and its
  complexity.

### 9. FAQ

Objections, answered briefly.

- **Do you build on Shopify or other platforms?** We build custom ecommerce, so
  the store fits your products and the way you sell rather than a template's
  limits.
- **What do you need from us to create the 3D?** CAD or STEP files are ideal.
  Photos, drawings, or a physical sample also work.
- **Can the configurator handle our pricing rules and connect to our ERP?** Yes.
  We build pricing and quoting around your rules and connect to your ERP where
  you need it.
- **We already have a store. Can you rebuild it without losing sales or search
  rankings?** Your current store keeps selling until launch. We carry over
  products and the pages that already rank, and redirect old links.
- **How long does a project take?** 1–3 months, depending on your product range
  and its complexity.
- **What does it cost?** Projects start at $10k. After the first call you get a
  fixed proposal.
- **Do you stay on after launch?** Yes: conversion testing, SEO, campaigns, and
  new product drops.
