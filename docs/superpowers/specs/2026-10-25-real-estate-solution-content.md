# Real Estate: solution page content strategy

The [rendering and configuration audit](2026-10-25-real-estate-content-audit.md)
updates the heading, section order, visualization coverage, and project previews.
That audit and the implemented page take precedence over the original copy below.

Prepared: 25 October 2026

Status: implemented at `/services/real-estate` (the old `/services/property-management`
URL redirects there). The selector demo uses illustrative sample data, and
images are placeholders until approved project media arrive.

## Name and positioning

**Solution name: Real Estate**

Use this short label in the Solutions menu and cards. It covers development
marketing, property sales, and property management. Keep the page heading more
specific: **Real estate websites, 3D visuals, and software.**

Help developers, homebuilders, real estate brands, and property teams present
properties, support sales enquiries, and manage daily operations. Connect the
creative work with the software needed to run it, through one in-house team.

Three needs should be clear from the opening:

- Present a development, including properties that are not built yet.
- Help buyers explore spaces, select a property, and make an enquiry.
- Manage listings, sales enquiries, and ongoing property operations.

Keep the language descriptive and concise, following the revised ecommerce page.
Explain what clients receive and what it helps their customers or staff do.
Use capability names as short supporting labels, with detailed explanations on
their own pages.

### 3. Interior and exterior visuals

**H2:** Interior and exterior visuals

**Body:** Help buyers understand the architecture, layouts, finishes, and setting
before they visit or construction is complete.

Three short supporting items:

- **Show the development.** Exterior and aerial views explain buildings,
  landscaping, amenities, and the surrounding area.
- **Present the interiors.** Room views show layouts, materials, lighting, and
  furnishing options.
- **Let buyers explore.** Web walkthroughs and 360° tours help buyers view spaces
  remotely. Add a dedicated VR experience where useful.

**Visual:** A small exterior/interior sequence with a walkthrough preview.
Use the same project across the sequence so visitors can understand the connection.
Supporting labels: Architectural visualization · Animation · Web tours · VR.

### 4. Interactive plot and unit selection

**H2:** Interactive plot and unit selection

**Body:** Let buyers explore a development, choose a building or floor, and review
available plots or apartments. Show layouts, sizes, and relevant property details,
then capture an enquiry for the selected property.

**Visual:** A rendered master plan or building view with selectable areas and a
property information panel. Demonstrate the actual sequence: development →
building or plot → floor or unit → details → enquiry.

Pre-rendered visuals with interactive overlays are an appropriate option; do not
describe every selector as a real-time 3D environment. Pricing and availability
can be connected to approved property records where required. Do not imply live
updates without the relevant data connection.

**Supporting labels:** Interactive maps · Unit selectors · Web apps.

### 5. Websites for property enquiries

**H2:** Websites for property enquiries

**Body:** Give your brand or development a clear online presence. Bring property
details, floor plans, visuals, and tours together, with simple ways to request
information or arrange a viewing.

**Show three practical benefits:**

- Clear property information and mobile-friendly navigation.
- Enquiry forms that identify the property the buyer is interested in.
- Connections to the tools your sales team uses for follow-up.

**Visual:** A property page on desktop and mobile, showing the information and
enquiry route together. Keep branding and web development as supporting services,
rather than separate long capability sections.

### 6. Listings, sales, and property management

**H2:** Listings, sales, and property management

**Body:** Manage property records, listings, enquiries, and daily tasks in
connected tools. Give staff, tenants, and owners the access they need, with web
and mobile interfaces suited to their roles.

Use two groups to make the operational offer clear:

- **Listings and sales:** Property details, availability, sales enquiries, and
  follow-up tracking.
- **Property operations:** Tenant and owner access, maintenance requests, rent
  and payment tracking, and reporting.

These modules reflect the offer in the brief and the existing property-management
solution content. Confirm the modules for each engagement; do not imply every
project needs or includes all of them. Describe the solution through its functions,
without naming the underlying software product or suggesting every module is
developed from scratch.

**Proof:** Include an approved management-system screenshot or a short, approved
UAE/USA client example here when available. Acknowledge anonymisation if client
details are withheld. Do not invent an unnamed case study, operational figures,
or results. The named visualization projects above are separate evidence.

### 7. One in-house team

**H2:** One team for visuals, websites, and software

**Body:** Our 3D artists, designers, and developers work together on your project.
You have one point of contact for the property visuals, website, and management
tools, with support after launch.

Keep the process in a compact four-step row:

1. **Review:** Understand the development, buyers, property data, and team workflows.
2. **Plan:** Agree the required visuals, features, integrations, budget, and timeline.
3. **Create and build:** Produce the visuals and develop the agreed website and tools.
4. **Launch and support:** Test the experience, prepare the team, and support updates.

Avoid importing the ecommerce budget, timeline, or free concept offer. None has
been specified for this solution.

### 8. Questions and project enquiry

**H2:** Discuss your real estate project

**Body:** Share your development, current website, or management requirements.
We’ll review what you need and discuss the scope, budget, and launch plan.

**CTA:** Discuss your project

Link to the existing contact page. A secondary WhatsApp link can use the existing
contact details. Keep the same primary CTA throughout the page.

Keep five FAQs short:

- **Can you work from architectural drawings?** Yes. We can work from drawings
  or existing 3D models. We confirm the files and material references needed
  during planning.
- **Do buyers need a VR headset?** Web tours are designed for browser access.
  A headset is only needed for a dedicated VR experience.
- **Can availability connect to our sales system?** We review your property data
  and integration options, then agree how availability will be updated.
- **Can we use only part of the service?** Yes. The scope can cover visuals, a
  website, an interactive selector, management tools, or a combination.
- **What will it cost and how long will it take?** This depends on the development,
  visual assets, features, and integrations. We agree the budget and timeline
  after reviewing your requirements.

## Design consistency

- Follow the existing ecommerce page: sky-blue hero, white content areas, dark
  feature sections, blue accents, rounded media, and the shared typography.
- Reuse the header, footer, container width, eyebrow labels, pill links, media
  cards, FAQ pattern, and existing motion rules. Keep reduced-motion support.
- Show architectural work early. Use a large master-plan example and fewer,
  well-captioned images rather than a long wall of capability cards.
- Give the selector and management section distinct visual space. Buyers should
  understand that one helps customers choose properties and the other helps
  staff run them.
- Use preview images for external experiences. Load an embedded or full demo
  only when requested, with a visible link to open the client site.
- Support interactive maps with searchable or filterable property lists on mobile
  and for keyboard users. Property selection must not depend on hover or colour alone.
